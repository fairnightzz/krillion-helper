KRILLION · HELPER

When off, the helper does nothing: no typing, submitting, overlay, or answer fetching.

Mode applies to the solo dive and to Puffer Party alike, rated and casual: “Fill and submit automatically” types and submits, “Fill the box” types only and you press Enter, “Only show suggestions” shows candidates only. In Party the helper types human-like, one keystroke at a time, when it is your turn. Word choice applies to both games: Common only uses the safe bench in party games and all-common answers in the solo dive. Words are always typed in lowercase.

The key is stored only in this browser profile (chrome.storage.local) and sent only to api.anthropic.com.
